Program Details by Age Group

A closer look at what each stage focuses on and how sessions are structured.

5–7 Years · Foundation

Early years focus on building a strong, confident base through play, stories, and natural interaction with authentic American English.

  • Phonics & early reading: Letter-sound connections, blending, and first books in a fun, low-pressure way.
  • Speaking & listening: Everyday vocabulary, simple conversations, songs, and confidence to use English.
  • School readiness: Support for classroom language, following instructions, and early writing skills.
  • Approach: Short, engaging activities tailored to attention span and interests. Progress is gentle and visible.

8–11 Years · Comprehensive

Primary years build balanced skills across all areas so school English feels more manageable and enjoyable.

  • Reading comprehension: Understanding stories and texts, vocabulary growth, and discussing ideas.
  • Writing & grammar: Clear sentences, short paragraphs, common structures, and accuracy without overload.
  • Speaking & confidence: Expressing opinions, describing, and reducing hesitation in English.
  • School support: Homework help, school topics, and strategies that transfer to class.
  • Approach: Mix of guided practice and independent work, adjusted weekly based on progress.

12+ Years · Advanced

Secondary and older students receive targeted support for academic demands, exams, and real communication.

  • Academic writing: Essays, structure, clarity, and developing stronger arguments.
  • Exam techniques: Reading strategies, time management, and common question types (school tests or public exams).
  • Advanced language: Wider vocabulary, natural phrasing, and more sophisticated speaking.
  • Confidence & independence: Tools students can use on their own between sessions.
  • Approach: Goal-oriented sessions with clear focus areas and regular feedback on progress.

Test Preparation

Targeted support for major exams and assessments relevant to Hong Kong students.

HKDSE

English Language papers — reading, writing, listening, and speaking. Focus on question techniques, time management, and authentic language use.

IB Diploma

Support for IB English assessments, essays, oral work, and related components. Sessions focus on analysis, structure, and the language skills needed for strong results.

IELTS

Academic and General Training. Band-score strategies for Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking with authentic practice and feedback.

TOEFL iBT

Integrated skills practice for university applications. Emphasis on academic reading, listening, writing, and speaking tasks.

SAT

Evidence-Based Reading and Writing section. Vocabulary in context, command of evidence, and rhetorical skills for US university applications.

Cambridge English

KET, PET, FCE, CAE, and CPE. Structured preparation for Cambridge exams popular with international schools and universities.

Also available: school internal exams, TSA (primary), ACT, and other assessments. Sessions are tailored to the specific exam format and your child’s target score or grade.
